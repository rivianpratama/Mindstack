## Lens: Product generativity and Barnum stress-test: simulated LLM report generation for two novel profiles (flat/compressed k=1

**Verdict:** Conceptually this is the best-armored typology product design I have seen — falsifiers, cost quotas, contrast quotas, and honest-null handling are real engineering, not tone — but as a generation spec it is not yet executable, and its failure points cluster exactly where the two test profiles live. Blockers: the friction map's tier vocabulary does not compose with 02's smear machinery (04 already violates 02 on the flagship example to compensate); the flat-profile pipeline is deadlocked between honest-null rules and hard per-section quotas, with 04 defaulting to an all-flow horoscope; and the docs' own examples run on an unfired 'S3-adjacent' rule, teaching the generator that detection gates are soft. Majors: no compendium content exists for mid-tier functions (the majority of most profiles), eruption candidacy is defined incompatibly across four docs, weak-signal geometry has three conflicting definitions, the mirror test deletes its own skeleton's best sentence, and dynamics prose is shape-generic — so reports will differentiate across shape classes but converge to templates within them, amplified by a single worked example threaded through all five documents. Fix the tier/edge-window contract, unify eruption and weak-signal definitions, add mid-tier compendium blocks and two contrasting fully-worked profiles, and replace felt-experience fork markers with behavioral discriminators; with those done, the framework plausibly clears the Barnum bar it sets for itself.
